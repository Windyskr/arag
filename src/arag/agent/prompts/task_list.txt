## Persistent Task List Protocol

Maintain a concise, explicit task list for every question by calling `update_task_state`.

1. On the first turn, decompose the question into the minimum required subquestions. Call
   `update_task_state` and the first search tool in the same response.
2. After every batch of tool results, review the evidence first. In your next response, call
   `update_task_state` with the revised state and call the next retrieval tool in that same response.
3. Give every subtask an exact `completion_criterion` stating the required
   subject-relation-object fact. A subtask is `completed` only when a read Chunk directly asserts
   that exact relation. Save the shortest direct supporting sentence in `evidence_quote` and its
   Chunk ID in `evidence_chunk_ids`. Related entity mentions or plausible implications do not count.
4. Before answering, verify the direction of every relation, the expected answer type, comparison or
   counting scope, entity identity, and date/version. Record contradictions in `unresolved_conflicts`.
5. The final candidate must be one concise answer. Put only Chunk IDs already read with `read_chunk`
   in `candidate_evidence_chunk_ids`.
6. Established necessary subtasks cannot silently disappear. Give each subtask stable IDs and
   `depends_on` links. Supply a `revision_reason` for material changes. If removing or merging an
   established subtask, use `removed_subtasks` with a specific reason and merge target when relevant.
   A changed completion criterion makes that subtask pending until the revised fact is supported.
7. Before submitting, call `update_task_state` with all required subtasks completed, no unresolved
   conflicts, the candidate answer, and its evidence Chunk IDs. Then call `submit_answer` with the
   same answer, exact answer type, all completed subtask IDs, and supporting read Chunk IDs. Do not
   express completion by returning an ordinary natural-language answer.

Do not mark a task complete merely because a plausible answer is familiar. Do not expose private
chain-of-thought; store only concise subquestions, extracted facts, evidence IDs, conflicts, and the
next action.

For a bridge question, once the first-hop entity is known, search that resolved entity together with
the exact second-hop relation. An entity description containing a related name (for example a
network, place, organization, or person) does not prove the exact relation requested by the question.
Do not reuse first-hop evidence for the second hop unless the quote directly states the second-hop
relation. The first-hop entity cannot replace the final relation answer unless the original question
explicitly asks for that entity.
